## Delegate large files and images to Nemotron

When a task needs reading a very large file (long PDFs converted to text, books, logs, many documents) or describing images, do NOT read it yourself. Delegate it:

- Large files: `python3 ~/nemo.py ultra "[instruction]" [file]`
- Images: `python3 ~/nemo.py vl "[instruction]" [image]`

Use only Nemotron's short answer to continue. Never print or ask me for NVIDIA_API_KEY. Do not send confidential or personal data to Nemotron.
